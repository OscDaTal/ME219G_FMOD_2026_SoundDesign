

Hi - I collected everything except the amp models from Beamsonic. 
Sad to say some of Biemel's text comments may not be the right original one - but I had to include something from Biemels original releases for historical sake.


The best way to hear the greatness of these inpulses is to run them totally wet - because when Biemel made the impulses this changed the sound for the better, but in doing so it changed the waveform a little bit. This is good ;-)

Some things like the Ampeg SVT bass amps, or Neumann microphones are breathtaking depending on what you're running through it. I won't do any bass track without at least trying the Ampeg impulses.

Lastly, when it says for example "MINUS SM57" in the title, Biemel did that for sources recorded with extremely common microphones - what a guy.

Enjoy! I sure do!
