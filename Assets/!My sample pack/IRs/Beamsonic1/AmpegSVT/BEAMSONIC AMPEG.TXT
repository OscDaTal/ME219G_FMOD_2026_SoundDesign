What can I say: Yes, I´m an addict .... to ACOUSTIC MIRROR of SONIC FOUNDRY. 
The great thing about this plug-in is, that you can create MIRRORS of your sound-equipment and you
can overlay or mix it to the file you like.
I mirrored nearly all my equipment, that I have collected now for years and .... 
these files are the result.

On Producing A MIRROR:
For best DA-AD conversion I used my T.C. Finalizer Plus. For amps- & microphone-mirrors I used my 
SPL GoldMike, like I would make a recording. For mirroring the microphones I used my GENELEC-Speaker
system, because they have a nearly flat response and even work at 30Hz.

Someone said: "You can´t match the original, because you´ll have to get about 1000 mirrors to come 
close to what you can do with a JoeMeek or a Marshall or a UREI." 
but I have to say: the sound is very, very similar.


!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

On Using The Studio-Mirrors: You´ll get no compression effect, you´ll get the sound-character of 
	the original device when the effect works. You´ll have to use a different compressor: I´ll 
	recommend to use Waves RCL or Timeworks Compressor-X.

On Using The Amp-Mirrors: You´ll get no distortion effect. Try distortion with Steinberg FuzzBox 
	or QuadraFuzz or RedValve IT and then overlay the mirror of the amp you´d like.
	WOW, you´ll be amazed!!!
	If you´d like record your guitar or bass go direct with a little analog (or t.c.finalizer)
	compression.
	Download the Testfiles: "BASSandGTR.zip" (inluding a recorded 1968 Fender Jazz-Bass, 6120
	Gretsch, 1962 Fender Stratocaster, 1980 Gibson LesPaul Heritage) from my SITE for testing!

On Using the Microphone Mirrors: I included some MINUS-FILES. These files first take away the 
	character of a SM58 or SM57 and overlays the character of the microphone you´ll choose. 
	Otherwise you´ll get a soundmix-character of SM57/58 and the one you´ll choose and ....
	that won´t come close to the sound of the original microphone.
	Download the Vocal-Testfiles: "VocalSM57andSM58.zip" from my SITE for testing!

!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

General Information:

You´ll have to use compression or distortion first, then normalize the sound to -1dB, because SONIC
FOUNDRY say: "Then you´re sure there will be no digital software distortion." 

Then use the ACOUSTIC MIRROR Plug-In, set it to the highest resolution, which is setting 
quality-speed = 5, then you´ll get the best results. 
You can adjust the fader "APPLY ENVELOPE AND LIMIT DECAY TO", that there is no dropout or 
CPU-overload. Adjust the WET OUT fader, so that there is no data over 0dB.
(I recommend to set the dry out to inf. for best results, but feel free to experiment)
