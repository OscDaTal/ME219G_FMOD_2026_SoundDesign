I´m an addict to Sonic Foundry´s Acoustic Mirror. 
The great thing about this plug is, that you can create MIRRORS of your sound-equipment 
and you can overlay it to the one you choose. 
I did these with all my equipment, I collected now for years and these are the files:
I did this with my T.C. Finalizer Plus for best DA-AD conversion. 
You can´t match the original, because you´ll have to get about 1000 mirrors to come close 
to what you can do with a JoeMeek or a Marshall or a UREI and ..... 
but the sound is very, very similar.

 

You´ll have to use compression or distortion first, the normalize the sound to -1dB, 
Sonic Foundry says then you´re sure there will be no digital software distortion. 
Then use the SONIC FOUNDRY Acoustic Mirror Plug-In, set it to the highest resolution, 
which is setting quality-speed = 5, then you´ll get the best results. 
Adjust the WET OUT fader, so that there is no data over 0dB 
(I recommend to set the dry out to inf. for best results, but feel free to experiment)
