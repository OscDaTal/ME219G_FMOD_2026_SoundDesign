Dear users of the Buiksloterkerk IR,

First i hope you will enjoy these impulse responses i've made.

This IR was captured when i was at this place for a film shoot. Of course i couldn't resist IR'ing it. I used a pair of Sonodore mic's for the LR reverb wich are excellent omni microphones and i had two Schoeps CMC5 MK5 (omni) which i used for the rear channels. The IR can be used as mono to stereo (1-2) or as mono to quad. As time is short on film shoots i used my starter gun for capture.

The IR's are freeware, but i will be most honoured when you tell me you used them for your projects so please don't hestitate to let me know.  Ofcourse, i will listen to the cd's you all will send me on wich you used my IR's. But the main reason i distribute my IR's is that i hope others will do the same.
