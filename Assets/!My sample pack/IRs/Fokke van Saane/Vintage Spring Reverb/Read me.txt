To all users of the Vintage Spring Reverb IR.

I hope you will enjoy this very typical spring reverb IR.

A friend of mine, Jef Nassenstein, digged up this old unit and it was still working. I have no idea about the brand. He spoke about Grundig, but i cannot see the name anywhere. The spring itself has no brand, most electronic parts are Siemens. After the capture i replaced the spring in this unit for an old spring i once took out of an old broken organ.  My 'organ' spring is the same size and has the name Gibbs on it, related to Hammond.

I made two mono IR's , and combined the two as a stereo couple to make a sort of fake stereo.

The IR's are freeware, but all i ask is that if you make a nice cd with it you owe me a copy!

You can reach me at fokkie@xs4all.nl for all comments, both negative and positive.

Have fun!

Fokke van Saane
