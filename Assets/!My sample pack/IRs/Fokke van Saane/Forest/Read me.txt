Dear users of the Forest IR

Since many years, long before sampling reverb was possible, i wanted to make the echo of a forest. The Lexicons and EMT plates where just not able to give me that. I always wondered why this forest reverb/echo is so special that no unit could make that. I still am.

I am not saying that i am fully satisfied, but for a little i am. These three IR's come close to what i have in mind. They are intended to use in film and tv postproduktion where you need the sound of a forest. I wanted to make a man shouting from far in the woods in my studio. This is possible with this IR, in my opinion. But i am very curious if it will work for you. We have to find out in real life.

This IR is free, but, please, if you use it, or hoped that you could use it but in vain; please let me know. Tell me why, or why not. I will keep recording IR's and need your comments in order to learn how they work out for you in real life and what i can do to improve them.

Please email me at fokkie@xs4all.nl
Positive or negative, i can handle it.
